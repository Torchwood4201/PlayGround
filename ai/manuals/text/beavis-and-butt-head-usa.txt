Playing Tips 


Try using the Button A leg-throwing move in a variety of 
areas. It may give you access to special food power-ups or 
help you get by tricky areas. 


Take the time to interact with the different background 
objects. Sometimes they'll contain valuable coins or 
defensive tools, 


Although you can initially play any level you wish (besides 
GWAR), first-time players may want to begin at the Streets 
of Highland.
