In addition to all the basic attacks

Super KOs 

Knockout punches are also a very real part of life in the clay 
fighter arena. If you are able to hit your opponent with a num- 
ber of successive hits while not getting hit yourself, then the let- 
ters KO will flash on the screen. As soon as the KO appears, you 
can knock the clay out of your opponents with a Super KO 
Attack. To do this, attack the opposing clay fighter by using any 
special move that is not a projectile. Watch out though! If your 
opponent is not stunned when the KO flashes, you will lose 
your ability to administer this attack if he hits you.
