Setting The Hook 

When the bass strikes, you will 
hear a snapping sound. 
Immediately set the hook by 
pressing A. You will know the 
bass is hooked by the line 
tension sound and the line 
tension meter, 


Monitor the tension meter carefully, if the gauge 
reaches the end and turns red, your line will 
‘snap. A warning will sound if your line is in 
danger of snapping. 


Landing A Bass
