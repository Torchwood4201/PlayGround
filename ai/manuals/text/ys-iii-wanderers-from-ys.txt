Our tale takes place after Adol and his

friend, Dogi, have completed a harrowing adven-
ture in which they conquered an evil force and
saved the land of Ys from doom.

‘As the pair continues to journey about the
land of Ys, they come upon a town being visited
by a gypsy caravan. While talking to the Lowns-
people, the two friends hear about Felgana.
Evidently, mysterious weather, bad crops, and
rising prices have made it a bad place for mer-
chants to do business.

Dogi was worried. His hometown, Redmont, was
in the heart of Felgana. His uncasiness is heightened
when he has his fortune told by a beautiful fortune-
teller The fortune-teller gazes into her crystal ball,
but as Dogi's future is being revealed, the crystal
ball explodes!! The explosion leaves the fortune-
teller unconscious. Something mysterious was hap-

pening!
Adol had a nagging premonition, "Let's go to

adventure in the land of Ys...
