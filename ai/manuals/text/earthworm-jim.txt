Plasma Power: Each time you pick up one of these you get an additional
250 rounds of plasma power. Plasma shots are limited so get as many of

Fuel Pods: While racing, grab these items for fun and profit. Better than sell-
ing Grit (America’s Family Newspaper) and much easier.

Extra Life: Hidden throughout the vast levels are these items that will give you
an extra life towards rescuing the princess.
