INTRODUCTION

P.T.O. is a WWII simulation on the Pacific. Relive this theater
of war by either taking the side of the Japanese or Allied
forces. As Naval Commander, you may control as many as
16 fleets. With direct control over the #1 Fleet, formulate a
winning strategy to full your military objectives.

Nine scenarios make up the entire Pacific Campaign with
‘each one requiring specific goals for victory. Major battles at
Pearl Harbor, Coral Sea, Midway, Solomon islands, Marianas
islands, Philippine Sea and Okinawa are realistically simulat-
ed. Victory in one scenario will allow you to continue the
game to the end, Ultimately, your goal is to either destroy all
of the enemy’s ships or occupy all 45 seaside bases.

Air, land and sea operations are at your disposal. Orders
such as a landing invasion or the bombing of a base can be
part of your overall strategy. Rely on history proven tech
riques or develop your own. Whatever your strategy-make
sure victory is:on your side Of the Pacific!
