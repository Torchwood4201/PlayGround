If you select any of the three courses, 
you'll play the Time Attack. If you 
clear the course in under two minutes,

against it. While best times will be 
saved, the star will disappear if you 
play another game.

Basic Rule 4 


The object of the game is to collect as

handy, too.

be displayed. There are 60 different 
treasures. See if you can find all of them!
