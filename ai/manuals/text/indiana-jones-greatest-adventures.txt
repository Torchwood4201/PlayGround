WEAPONS

Along with your fists, you have several weapons available to you.
But you must find them first. Weapons can be found in satchels or
clay pots scattered around the game. To pick up a weapon, simply
walk into it. If you fail to complete a level, when you begin again,
you will lose all your weapons—except for a grenade (you always
start with one grenade).

PISTOL:

Fires a fast single shot.

GRENADE:

Causes a blast that destroys every enemy on the screen.
You begin with one grenade.

Wuip:

Perfect for slashing foes and helping you swing over lava pits,
piranha pools and other seemingly impossible obstacles.
