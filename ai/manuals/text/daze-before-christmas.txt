===== Page 4 =====

Insert your Daze Before Christmas game cartridge into your
Super Nintendo Entertainment System and switch the Power
ON. After the title screen appears you have three different
selections. Push left or right for either a One Player or Two
Player Game. The middle selection opens the Option Menu.

In the Option Screen you can change a number of settings.
Difficulty (Easy,Medium and Hard), Music and Sound Effects,
the controls for in game and you can also enter a PASSWORD
here to carry on from a certain level in a previous game.

===== Page 6 =====

Are the Children all over the world to experience a huge
dissapointment this Christmas? Not if Santa can help it!

He is helped by four lives and unlimited continues in order

to find all the presents and deliver them to the various houses
everywhere before Christmas Day. In order to bring the gifts
he must be able to fend off the cursed toys by either throwing
magic dust at them or by jumping on them. Once defeated
they will transform back into harmless toys and are wrapped
up in Blue gift paper ready for Santa to collect. Besides these
are Ked and Gold wrapped presents from which Santa can get
different Pick-Ups. Some of them contain great suprises. For
example: Additional lives, Gold Bells which serve as mid-level
checkpoints, Hats for Santa’s Health bar and maybe even a
missing Elf will jump out of them. But Bewarel In certain ones
you will find Bombs or an Enemy waiting for you....

Can you possibly believe that such a good hearted man like
Santa Claus would have trouble saving the day. Unfortunatley
you will very soon realize that even the good have weaknesses.
If Santa drinks a cup of Tea he turns into the Anti-Claus.
Instead of defeating the enemy with his magic dust Anti-Claus
has only his sack as a weapon, but this also destroys presents.
Not to worry though as the effect of the Tea does not last long
and then Santa can continue on his way collecting all the
presents ready for Christmas Day.
