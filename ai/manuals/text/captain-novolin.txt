Notice to Parents/User

This game is an educational tool to show children with diabetes how to
balance diet, exercise and insulin therapy. Insulin is a drug product
which must be used carefully and only under medical supervision.
Therefore you should never adjust or change insulin therapy, including
your brand of insulin, without first consulting your doctor. Show him
the notice below.
