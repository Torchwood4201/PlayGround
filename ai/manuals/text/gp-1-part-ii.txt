There are five adjustable features : PAD ASSIGN-

= =( Pad Assignment )—=

Use the four controller set-
tings to change the position
of the throttle, brake and
other functions. A is the

——e Level )}—

There are three levels. In EASY, you will not lose control or
crash if you hit another bike. In NORMAL, if you hit another
machine you will lose control briefly but will not crash. If you
choose HARD, you will lose control if you bump another rider
and will crash if you hit him hard.

—C Sound ==

You may choose either Mono or Stereo to suit your television

or sound system.
