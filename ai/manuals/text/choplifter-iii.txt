PLAYING THE GAME

Once you have made your selections from the
options screen you will be taken into the briefing room. The
top half of the screen is a large projector screen on which a
map will be displayed. On the bottom half of your screen is
your commander who is briefing you on your mission.

The map is an overview of all four sectors that you
have to rescue the hostages from in order to finish the game.

The sectors on the map can be zoomed in on to give you a
closer look at the area you will be entering.

LIVES

The options screen allows you to select the number
of lives you would like for the game. If this is not selected,
the game will automatically default to 4 lives.

Each time your damage bar runs down to zero, a life
will be lost and you will begin the level again. _
