“You can’t let the score break your

GAME POINTS 


FOR EACH PLAYER: 

The first point won scores 15. The second point won increases the 
score to 30, and the third point increases it to 40. The fourth point 
wins the game, unless the previous score was Deuce. The game 
must be won by two points. 


Note: You ace your opponent when you score a point on your 
serve, without your opponent being able to return it. 


FOR BOTH PLAYERS: 

Scores are announced after each point, with the server's score first. 
If che server wins che first point in a game, che score is 15-0, If che 
receiver wins the next point, che score becomes 15 All. Then, if che 
receiver also wins the third point, the score is 15-30. 


DEUCE: 

A score of 40-40 is Deuce. The next point won gives che. 
advantage to the scorer. For example, ас Deuce, if George King is 
serving and wins che point, the score is Advantage King, or Ad 
In. On his next serve, if the receiver, Agassi, wins che point, the 
score is Deuce again. When Agassi also wins the next point, che 
score becomes Advantage Agassi, or Ad Out. 

А player who scores on an advantage wins the game. In other 
words, when the score is Deuce, a player must take two points in a 
row co win the game. 


SET 


The first player to win six games wins the set. The exception is 
when the score is 6-5. Then a player must win by two games to 
take the set. Examples of set-winning scores аге 6-2 and 7-5. 


If the score becomes 6-6, a tiebreaker is played. The service goes to 
the player who received the serve last. This player serves once. 
Then the service switches to the other player for 2 points. The 
serve continues to alternate after every 2 points. The first player to 
reach 7 points wins the set; however, that player must win by 2 
points. 


The last point needed to win a set is the set point. 


MATCH 


The match goes to the male player who wins three out of five sets, 
or to the female player who wins two out of three sets. 


A match can last for as short as two sets (if a female player wins 
them both), or for as long as five sets (if each male player wins two 
of the firse four sets). A match is never longer chan five sets in 
Andre Agassi Tennis 


Note: In Exhibition mode, you can choose to play a single set (1

The final point needed to win a match is the match point.
