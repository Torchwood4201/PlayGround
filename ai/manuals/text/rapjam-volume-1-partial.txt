1 PLAYER MODE
When you are the only human player on your team
(the other players are controlled by the CPU), you
can choose between Solo (default) and Spirit
modes. In Solo mode, the player controls only the
teammate he chooses. In Spirit mode, the player
controls the teammate who has the ball. You'll see
your controller number under this teammate's feet.

It's not necessary to touch the ball to change team-
mates since the teammate you control is always the
teammate closest to the ball.
