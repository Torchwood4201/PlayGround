Special Moves 


As you proceed in the adventure, you will meet old hermits 
who will teach Master Higgins useful new tricks. These 
tricks will prove helpful and necessary to complete the 
game. Master Higgins will learn three special tricks in the 
following order.

Shove - Once you learn this trick you will be able to move objects by just standing next to it 
and pushing the Control Pad in the direction you wish to move the object 


Down Jab - You will be able to break blocks with triangles which point down on them, once 
you learn this trick, To Down Jab, jump up, push the Control Pad down then push the Y- 
Button when coming down 


Up Jab - With this smashing blow, like the Down Jab, you'll be able to break the blocks 
with triangles which point up on them. Jump up with the Control Pad pushed up and push 
the Y-Button to destroy the blocks. 


With these three tricks, you'll be able to reach difficult areas, But remember, nothing comes 
free. These three hermits will ask for some hard cash.Sso remember to have some on
