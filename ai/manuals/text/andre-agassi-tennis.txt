GAME POINTS

FOR EACH PLAYER:

The first point won scores 15. The second point won increases the
score to 30, and the third point increases it to 40. The fourth poine
wins the game, unless the previous score was Deuce. The game
must be won by two points.

Note: You ace your opponent when you score a point on your
serve, without your opponent being able co return it.

FOR BOTH PLAYERS:

Scores are announced after each point, with the server's score first.
If che server wins the first point in a game, the score is 15-0. If che
receiver wins the next point, che score becomes 15 All. Then, if the
receiver also wins the third poine, the score is 15-30.

Deuce:

A score of 40-40 is Deuce. The next point won gives the
advantage to the scorer. For example, at Deuce, if George King is
serving and wins the point, the score is Advantage King, or Ad
In. On his next serve, if the receiver, Agassi, wins che point, the
score is Deuce again. When Agassi also wins the next point, the
score becomes Advantage Agassi, or Ad Out.

A player who scores on an advantage wins the game. In other
words, when the score is Deuce, a player must cake two points ina
row co win the game.
