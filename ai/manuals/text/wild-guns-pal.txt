For greater enjoyment of the game, please take the time to read this manual. 
The following items are for the care of your game. 


1. Avoid subjecting the Game Pak to extreme temperature changes and 
shocks. 

2. Do not touch the terminal connectors. Keep them safe by storing the game 
in the protective storage case. 

3. The use of solvents, thinners, alcohol, benzene and other strong agents can 
damage the Game Pak. 

4. Do not dismantle the Game Pak.
