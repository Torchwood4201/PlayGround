How to Start Play

Press SELECT at the title screen or move the cursor using the
up/down keys. Press the START button to enter the various
modes. (Pressing the START button during the demo returns
you to the title screen.)

Player 1...go to p.10

Note: During play, with the use of a 2P controller, a second
player can join play in the middle of the game.

Player 2...go to p.10
