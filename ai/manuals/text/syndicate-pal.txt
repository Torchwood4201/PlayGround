SPEED 


To make your agents move more quickly around the Mission Zone you can increase the

GROUP MODE 


Combine all your agents in real time during the mission using Group Mode. Press SELECT to 
activate Group Mode; all your agents’ Status Boxes are highlighted and they respond to Control

or frightening foursome on patrol. 

To de-activate Group Mode, press SELECT again. The agent who was the last active before 
Group Mode activation becomes the only one under direct Control Pad command.
