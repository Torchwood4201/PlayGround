This Game Pak uses a program which randomly accesses the
puzzles. When your Super Nintendo Entertainment System is turned off,
the entire question file is cleared. The categories and the'answers” are
chosen at random by the computer, so although there are over 3,500
“answers” in over 700 categories, repetition may occur: If,
