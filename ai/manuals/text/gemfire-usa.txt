"Command which unit?"

unit and press C. The unit will highlight. 


"Move where?" 
Move the cursor to direct the unit's path of movement, ending in an 
open space. Then press C to move. 


"Which command?" 
If you want to move to a different space, press B now. Or, select a 
command with the cursor and press C. If you choose Wait, the unit 
will turn gray to indicate its turn is over. 


To Build or Break a fence, 
move the cursor [0 on open space adjacent to the unit, and press C. 
The unit's turn will automatically end. 


To Attack, 
move the cursor to an enemy unit in range, 
and press C. 


Viewing enemy's troops 
can be done before choosing a unit 
to command. Move the cursor to a unit and press C. 
Or, to view the battlefield, press A. 


Once you finish your turn, press B to access the 
war options menu. Choose Done to end.
