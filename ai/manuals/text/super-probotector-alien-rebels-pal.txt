Random Shooting 

With both L and R buttons held down, 
press the Y button to fire randomly. 
Guns in both hands fire while the player 
spins around. This function cannot be 
used, however, when riding the airbike 
or armoured car, or hanging from the 
wall or ceiling. 


Movement along the Ceiling and Walls 
The player can move while gripping 
walls, ceilings and the like. However, 
the player will fall if he or she jumps 
while hanging, so care must be taken. 
In order to descend safely from a wall, 
use the down key plus the B button. 
Shooting is possible, but not shooting 
while moving. 


Armoured Car 

The player can ride in an armoured car. 
The left/right keys move the car 
forwards and backwards, the Y button 
fires a large cannon, and the B button 
enables you to get out of the car.
