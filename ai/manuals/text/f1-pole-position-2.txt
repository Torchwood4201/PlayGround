TYRE

You can choose slick tires (dry weather) or rain tires and
determine the hardness of the rubber:

HARD: hard resistant rubber with little traction.

GEAR

Short gear ratios, which mean a faster acceleration but a lower

There are 7 different brake levels, from SOFT (very resistant

resistant).

of your car’s frame, from SOFT (soft, little traction but
resistant) to HARD (hard, not resistant but a lot of traction).

WING

There are 7 different levels to help you adjust your car's
aerodynamics, from MAX SPEED (the closest to your car's
maximum speed) to DOWN FORCE, (which gives your car
greater stability).
