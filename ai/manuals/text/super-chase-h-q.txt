If you're a hotrod and want to shift your sports car manually
with the L and R Buttons, choose MANUAL. In AUTOMATIC,

your car automatically shifts up, but you can downshift with L.

Select CONTROLLER with the Control Pad and a diagram

LEVEL

EASY until you become familiar with the vehicle. When you feel
ready, move up to NORMAL.
