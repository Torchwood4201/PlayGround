Defensive Gameplay 


Good defense has won many a game. Here are some 
tactics for you to use in your strategy. 


Stealing - You can't score if you don't have the Баш Take 
the initiative! 


Set Screens - Don't give the offense а direct route to the 
basket... make them go around you! 


Switch Player Control - Use the X button to switch control 
to the appropriate player; it is alway best to be in control 
of the player that is nearest to the ball. 


Rebound - Be waiting for the ball under the hoop when- 
ever а shot is taken, then go for the Fast Break! 


Defensive Fouls - Although slightly unsportsmantike, a 
foul at the critical moment can turn the momentum of a 
game in your team’s direction, as well as make you feel 
better!
