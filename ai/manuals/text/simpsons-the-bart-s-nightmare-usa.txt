Bart’s first stop, in the bottomless wading pool we 
call his mind, is Windy World. From here you can 
reach each of the 5 Other Worldly Worlds that hold 
the missing pages of homework. The trick is to dis- 
cover how to deal with this wacked-out world before 
the moving mail boxes, three-eyed fish, rolling 
heads, and other odd stuff put an end to your sleep 
by stealing your supply of sleep “Z’s”...leaving you 
wide awake with no homework and a big fat “F.”

points... and know what to trash. Check out the 
handy Windy World Guide on pages 6 + 7.

Other Worldly Worlds and get on with your quest to 
find the homework. Try anything! Do anything. 
Splat innocent passers-by with bubblegum. Spit 
watermelon seeds. Even change your physical 
appearance. Because in a dream, anything is possi- 
ble.....and in a nightmare, the weirder it is....the 
more possible it becomes. Figure that out!
