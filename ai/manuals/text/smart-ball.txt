Somewhere, there is a mystical land called Kyliria, where the people
have been living in peace and harmony for as long as anyone can
remember. The king, Geoffrey, is very wise and just and he is now
preparing to retire and pass the crown to his eldest son JERRY.
JERRY has just married his wife, Wendy, and is ready to assume the
responsibilities of leadership. But Geoffrey's other son, Tom, isjealous
of JERRY, and has enlisted the help of the Bad Wizard, and together
they have brought down a great curse upon JERRY. They have stolen
Wendy away and stripped JERRY of his humanity, reducing him to a
small ball. Now, he must slither his way to the Bad Wizard's castle to
return to his normal self and rescue Princess Wendy.
