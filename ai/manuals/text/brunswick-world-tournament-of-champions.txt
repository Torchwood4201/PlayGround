Long Tournaments

In the long format, there will be an initial squad of 64
bowlers- 1 human player and a random selection of
Brunswick pro and other professional bowlers.

Qualifying

Each player in the initial squad must now play 18
qualifying games (this number will vary slightly between
tournaments), divided into three 6-game blocks. Scores
are based on a par of 200. If a player rolls a 212, he or
She will score a 12. If a player rolls a 189, he or she

and the top 24 players will proceed to the next round.
