MOVING AROUND ON THE ISLAND

Use the Control Pad to move the character along
paths. When encountering a landmark icon, press
any Button to enter that destination.

Speciat CONTROLS

Flying (Side Scrolling)

Press the B Button to keep the character airborne.
Use the Control Pad to move around. Press the Y
Button to fire ammunition at enemies.

Swimming
The controls for swimming are the same as those
on land, except there is no need to jump.

Climbing Trees

To climb trees, use the B Button to jump into
them, and then continue to Press the B Button
to move up through the trees.
