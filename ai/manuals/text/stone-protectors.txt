Far away in a distant galaxy...

The Crystal Palace of Mythrandir is under siege! Good Princess
Opal’s valiant guardsmen are taken by surprise, and are no
match for the swarming hordes of Zok, the evil Lord Emperor of
Trolls.

Lord Zok’s reptilian Saurian Guards have breached the Palace
defenses and invaded the inner sanctum where the powerful
Great Crystal is hidden. If Zok can obtain the Great Crystal,
Mythrandir would be under his slimy control forever!

To keep it from Zok’s evil grasp, Princess Opal attempts to
transport the Great Crystal to another dimension... but Zok is
too fast! As the Great Crystal flies through the air to safety, Zok
grabs at it and seizes a piece of the Great Crystal! Zok has the
Black Onyx!

Fortunately, the other five pieces of the Great Crystal escapes
to... Earth! Seattle, Grunge capital of the world, to be exact,
where five aspiring young musicians are playing a gig at a local
Rock’n’Roll club. The Gemstones of the Great Crystal home in
on the five musicians and transform these ordinary dudes into
the STONE PROTECTORS!

As the Stone Protectors, these Rock’n’Roll Trolls have awesome
fighting and musical abilities. Use these powers to defeat the
evil Lord Zok’s army of Saurian Guards and return Princess Opal
to the Crystal Palace as the rightful leader of Mythrandir. Zok
has seized the Crystal Palace and converted it into his Throne
Room. You’ve got to Rock’n’Roll your way through Mythrandir to
the Crystal Palace, and keep the Gemstones away from Lord
Zok’s forces of evil! Good luck, dude, it’s the ultimate battle!

PICK UP ITEMS

Scattered throughout Mythrandir are various weapons and items
that the Stone Protectors can pick up. To pick up a weapon,
stand over it and press the A Button. Certain weapons can only
be picked up and used by certain Stone Protectors.

Sometimes items will appear after you trash an enemy or smash
a crate or barrel. Poke around to find these potentially valuable
items in your quest to defeat Lord Zok. Items are picked up
automatically by walking over them. If your Stone Protectors
character cannot pick up and use a pick up weapon, you will hear
a sound prompt when you press the A Button.

You can share your available Life Meter energy with your fighting
partners using this feature. This is especially useful when one of
you is down to their last Lives Left. You can use the Power
Transfer Mode during 1 Player + CPU and 2 Player Mode.

To access this function, press the Start Button to pause the
game. When the game is paused, use the L and R Buttons on
your Controller to transfer energy from character to character
(you'll see the Energy Meters change length).

To return to the game, press the Start Button.

Before each stage of Mythrandir you travel through, Princess Opal
will offer advice you can use in your battle against Zok and his
Saurian Guards. Listen to her, she’s one smart Babe, er, um... |
mean Royal Highness!

Keep practicing your Special Moves! You'll need these powerful
attacks to defeat Lord Zok and his horrible henchmen.

Use different combinations of punch and kick attacks. Some
Saurian Guards have weak spots where you should concentrate
your hits.

Don't use the Power Moves too often...they are devastating, but
they drain your energy every time you use them.

Try and pick up as many objects as possible. If you can collect
five different gem crystals in one stage, you can receive a special
bonus!

Watch those sneaky little Crystal Thieves! They'll follow you
around and steal crystals from right under your nose!

Rapidly press left and right on the Control Pad to break an
enemy's hold on you.

Try playing the game in different difficulty levels. Each difficulty
level offers different game endings...
