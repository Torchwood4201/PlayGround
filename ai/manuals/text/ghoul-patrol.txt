Each level contains 10 victims that you must rescue. After
rescuing the victims, you will then need to find the time portal to
advance to the next level.

This place is a real ghost town.
Floating eyes, possessed library
books, teenager-eating plants,
green goblins and garbage can
ghosts are everywhere, and they
are freaking people out—big
time!

You'll begin your ghoul hunt in
the library. Collect all the keys you find as they will open doors
for you. Also pick up different weapons to increase your
arsenal. And be sure to follow the “SAVE ME” and “THIS WAY”
cries along the way—this will lead you to the spooked victims
you need to rescue.
