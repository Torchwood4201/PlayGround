Massachusetts, Dr. James Naismith, rugby coach at the school, was giten

athletes could play between football and baseball seasons. Second, he 
was to find u way to keep order in a class of competitive young men who 
were getting bored with the usual exercises and gymnastics. 


He came up with the beginnings of a game that borrowed fron rugh: 
soccer, water polo and other popular sports of the day. The school janitor 
was asked to find some boxes to be used as goals, but, unable to find

Büsketball's first game on record took place on December 21, 1891,

basketball was begun. 


Minor competition stated almost immediately, but basketball wasn't

the game had changed considerably since Dr. Naismith’s original game. 
Dribbling had become the standard method of offense by 1896, Double

players on a team was settled at five, after experiments with seven and 
nine players proved to be unworkable,

Five Second Violation 


When inbounding the ball after a basket or a foul, the offensive ean 
has five seconds to throw the ball in. If you fail to inbound the ball within 
those five seconds, you will be charged with a five-second violation, and 
your team will lose possession of the ball 


Three Second Violation 


An offensive player can spend no more than three seconds in the key 
(the painted area under the basket), Standing in the key for more than 
three seconds is a violation, and your team will lose possession of the bo 


Ten Second Violation 


The offensive team must get the ball past the center court line with 
ten seconds from the time the ball is inbounded. Failure to cross the li 
time isa violation resulting in a loss of possession for the offensive tean 


Backcourt Violation 

Once an inbound ball has been brought across the center line, it 
cannot be taken back across the line by an offensive player without 
incurring a violation, A backcourt violation will result in a loss of 
possession for the offensive team. 


Offensive Foul (Charging) 


An offensive foul occurs when an offensive player runs into a 
defensive player who is "ser". A player who is "set" has both feet on the 
floor and is facing the player with the ball 


fensive fouls include blocking the player in possession of the ball, 
contact with the shooter, or hitting the ball-handler while 
igo steal. 


A player will be called for traveling if he leaves the ground to attempt 
a shot and lands without releasing the ball. A traveling violation will cost 
your team possession of the ball. 


Shot Clock Violation 


The offensive team has 45 seconds, staring when the ball is inbound, 
lo take a shot at the basket. The ball must leave the player's hands before 
the shot-clock buzzer sounds, and the ball must then hit the basket rim or 
go through the basket to reset the clock. The shot clock will keep running 
ifthe shot is blocked and is recovered by the offensive team.
