While you are moving your boat around, pay close attention to the DEPTH 
FINDER. This shows the water depth, the condition of the underwater 
terrain, the schools of fish and flora. 

An alarm will alert you to the location of fish. Use this information to choose the 
best casting spot. 

The L and R Button will move the DEPTH FINDER screen from Left to Right.

is located, simply press “L ог R” and the screen will automatically move to 
the other side. 

The DEPTH FINDER indicates the depth of 
the water from top to bottom marked by ‘Y’ 
or ‘A’. The dashed line indicates that the 
underwater terrain is more difficult, possibly 
with weeds or a stronger current.
