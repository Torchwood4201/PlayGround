Ultra Plays can be used by the six teams on the Ultra League, or any 
edited teams based on these six teams. Each team has a certain number

are used up. When all the Ultra Points are used up, that team will be 
unable to use any more Ultra Plays. The key is to use your Ultra Plays at 
the right time during a game. (Ultra Points are fully restored at the beginning 
of each game, even in a series or season.)
