FINDING WEAPONRY

Careless monsters often leave their weaponry behind.
Also look in chests; sometimes you'll find better
equipment. Some weapons cannot be bought; for
example, the Broad Sword and Broad Rod. Items like
these are only found in treasure chests.

ASSIGNING EQUIPMENT

Arm yourself and Aguro with the mightiest swords
and helmets you can find. The Wave Ring increases
your powers, and Aguro knows how to use the Hi-Daze
Ring effectively. Because of her sorcery, arm Lufia
with staffs and rods. Jerin uses bows and arrows. Both
of them need to wear berets and caps for head
protection.

BATTLE PREPARATION

Monsters usually attack quickly, giving you little time to prepare. And
once battle is joined, the action is too fast and furious for you to change
weaponry. Avoid trouble by getting into the habit of equipping new
items as soon as you obtain them.
