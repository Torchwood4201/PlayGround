MESSAGE TO PLAYERS

This Game Pak uses a program which randomly accesses the
puzzles.When your Super Nintendo Entertainment System is turned off,
the entire question file is cleared. The categories and the “answers” are
chosen at random by the computer, so although there are over 3,500
“answers” in over 700 categories, repetition may occur. If, during a

HINTS ON GAME PLAY

1 Words should be spelled as accurately as possible. The program
will allow for some minor misspellings. Be sure to check your “ques-
tions” before locking them in.

2 If your “answer” deals with a person, try to give his/her full
name; if applicable, a full title (ie. George Washington or Henry Vill).

3. If the “question” is number oriented, the program will allow for
the spelling of the number or its numeric equivalent.

4 Spacing between words is optional.

5 When entering the “questions,” the cursor can be accelerated by

pressing the desired direction on the Control Pad and Button X,
simultaneously.
