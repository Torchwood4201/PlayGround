STARTING LEVEL 


Allows you to begin on any from one 
through seven. On the higher levels, the ghosts 
move faster and you will see new mazes! 


EXTRA LIVES 
You get an extra life at 10,000 points. Additional 
extra lives are available at 50,000, 100,000 and 
300,000 points, but only if you select the mini, big 
or strange mazes. 


FRUITS 
Eat the bouncing fruits for bonus points. If you 
select the strange mazes, strange fruits will 
appear starting at level 8. Some of these aren't 
fruits at all, but they are worth big bonus points 
just the same!
