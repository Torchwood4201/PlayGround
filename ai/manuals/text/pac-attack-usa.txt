Sneak preview box to see where Pac-Man will go and place him in

m Think several moves ahead. Try to place ghosts in lines for 
Pac-Man to eat. 

m Don't think vertically! Lining up ghosts in vertical stacks makes it 
difficult to clear rows of blocks. Make horizontal or diagonal lines 
of ghosts for Pac to gobble and you'll have an easier time.
