Use the onside kick to attempt to quickly regain possession of

game you may want to try an onside kick.

takes over on offense.

To attempt an onside kick:
1. From kicking formation, press A to call up the audible
indicator.

team shifts to the right- or left-hand side of the ball.

5. Control Pad «> and press B again immediately to stop the
power bar.

gets under the ball, catches it, and begins to run up field. If the
kick is deep and he catches it in the end zone, he remains in

NO HUDDLE

When the clock is running down at the end of the half or, more
critically, at the end of the game, you can save time and bypass
the Play Calling screen. At the end of a play, you can get right
back on the line of scrimmage by running a no huddle offense.
To execute a no huddle play:
1. Immediately after the ref blows the play dead, press and
hold A. Your team rushes back to the line of scrimmage.

2. Press B to snap the ball. Your team runs the same play
it ran the previous down.

To run an audible from a no huddle play:
1. Immediately after the ref blows the play dead, press and
hold A. Your team rushes back to the line of scrimmage.

2. Press A to call up the audible indicator.

3. Press A, B, or Y to select the desired audible.
(Press X if you decide not to run an audible.)

4. Press B to snap the ball. Your team runs the selected audible.
