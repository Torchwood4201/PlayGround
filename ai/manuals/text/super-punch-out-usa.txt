ADVICE (HOW TO WIN THE MATCH) 


It isn't effective to throw punches without thinking, 
especially when fighting with the higher ranked fight- 
ers, Look for ways to score a knock out while lasing аз 
little energy as possible. 


| Watch your opponent's movements closely. Most oppo- 
nents will telegraph their intentions before attacking. 


Dodge & Punch and Duck & Punch are the most basic 
combinations, Try to avoid your opponent's punch and 
quickly throw a punch of your own. If your timing is 
perfect, your punch will often cause your opponent to 
become dizzy and drop his guard. This is the best 
chance to use punch combinations. 


Try to minimize the damage you receive. Try to build 
up your Power Meter and keep it at maximum so that 
уои can use а Knock Out Punch at any time, 


Do not be careless after you knock down your opponent 
When your opponent stands up, he might come at you 
with a special attack, 


Although you should be paying attention at all the 
times during the match, be extra careful when instruc- 
tions are called out from the opponent's corner man. 
The comments are different for each fighter. There are 
mainly two types of instructions, one is about how to 
fight, the other is about specific attacks, Some corner 
men will call out more than one instruction for their 
fighter. (However, no more than one instruction is dis- 
played at a time.) 


Try to fight with purpose, not by chance. When your 
opponent is knocked down in an unexpected way, or 
when your punch hits your opponent with unexpected 
timing, try the same attack many times again. You 
might find a pattern that will severely damage your 
opponent.
