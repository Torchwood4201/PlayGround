Yo! Dudes and dudettes! 

Welcome back to California - land of Fun and Games! 
Get ready for the ultimate sequel: California Games II. 
It’s time for “fun in the sun” as you play five totally new 
events, 


First, you'll try Hang Gliding at San Francisco's Fort 
Funston, overlooking the Pacific Ocean, where the most 
daring hang gliders “hang out.” Next, you'll face the 
challenge of outlaw Skateboarding down the most 
aggro-cultural California Aqueduct. Then, you'll hike up 
to the top of Mt. Shasta for a wildly out-of-control 
Snowboarding run (rumor has it you can see all the way 
to San Francisco on a clear dayl)...all the way back 
down to Lake Shasta. After that, you'll compete in the 
Santa Cruz Bodyboarding Invitational, Finally, you'll 
take your surfjet out into Monterey Bay for the Santa 
Cruz Jet Surfing Competition, 


Objectives 


California Games Il is a game for one to eight players. 
The object of the game is to score well in every event. 


$e 


The player with the most points at the end of a competi- 
tion joins the Most Excellent Dudes (or Dudettes) list. Zak 
is the guru who hands out the points. He lets you know 
how you're doing and tells you if you are boring him. 
Practice to improve your skills in each event, compete 
with your family and friends, and soon you could be the 
number one Most Excellent Dude (or Dudette). 


In competition, if you break the record for an event, 
California Games Il records your name and the new 
high score on the Most Excellent Dudes (or Dudettes) 
screen. Of course, once you turn off your Super NES, Zak 
becomes the Most Excellent Dude again. 


Most Excellent Hints 

For all stunts, repeating the stunt will gain you extra 
points, but, beware, Zak gets easily bored. Repeat the 
same stunt more than 3 times and you score low points. 


All events are timed. In Snowboarding, each of the 3 
sections (Black Diamond, Snow Bowl, Obstacle Course) 
are timed separately. Zak will warn you when time is 
low and will award no more points when time runs out. 


An extra bonus is awarded for completing the event.
