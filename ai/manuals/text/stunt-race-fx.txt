monster machine with huge tires that give great grip performance.

F-Type turns quickly and controls easily because of his strong
down-force. Once he accelerates, F-Type runs very fast. When he
crashes though, he suffers a lot of damage. F-Type is best used by
advanced players.

There are two ways to race this car. You can chance it and try
drifting with the L and R Buttons, or run safely controlling the gas
pedal to match the tire grip. His body is weak, so adjust the balance
of the car in mid-air using «and on the +Control Pad to land
safely. This is a very important technique.

"Down-force" is created at high speed by the wind and holds the
car to the ground.
