‘The Lawnmower Man has simultaneous two player option. Angelo's 
weapons will not harm Carla and vice versa. if you are both walking in 


the same direction the lagging player will catch up. If a player leaves

automatically disappear and reappear next to his or her partner without 
losing a life.
