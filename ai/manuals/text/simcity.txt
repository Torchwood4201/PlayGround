Gites (and SimCities) live on money, both collecting it and spending it.
Money is used for building and maintaining Roads and Railroads and to hire
Policemen and Firemen. Zoning land also costs money, as does building a
‘Sea Port or Stadium. With all this money being spent, cities need a lot of

money coming in. Much of it comes from taxes. In SimCity, your taxes are
based on population and Land Value. A large population living on valuable
property willpay the most tax dollars, so these aretwo of your goals—getting
‘big population and keeping Land Value high. Later in the game, you can
‘supplement your taxes with income from entertainment sources like the
Casino or Amusement Park.
