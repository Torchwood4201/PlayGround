24 Second Shot Clock

From the moment you gain possession of the ball, you have 24 seconds
to get off a shot that hits the rim. Each time the ball hits the rim or a
change of possession occurs, the clock is reset. The 24 second clock
appears at the bottom left of the screen when there are 10 or fewer
seconds left.
