EN WHAT 


Each match consists of 3 rounds. The first fighter to win 2 rounds is declared the winner 
and moves on to the next match. 


A round ends when a fighter's strength reaches zero, or when he or she can no longer 
stand up. The fighter still standing is declared the winner. 


If there is a fixed time limit, and both fighters have won the same number of rounds at 
the end of a match, the fighter with the most strength remaining is deciared the winner. 


In the event of a double knockout, or when both fighters have the same remaining 
strength level, the round ends in a draw. 


If neither fighter wins 2 out of 3 rounds , there is a final round to decide the winner. The 
winner of this round is declared the winner of the match. 


At the beginning of each round, the strength levels of both fighters are set back to 100%.
