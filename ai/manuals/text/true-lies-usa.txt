Options allows you to set up your TRUE LIES" game using 
the following choices: Difficulty, Sound and Controller.

password is completed. When END is highlighted, press A, 
B, X, or Y BUTTON to enter this selection. Once you've set 
yourself up, you're ready to take on your enemies in 
earnest. The screen for your mission will appear. 

Good luck, Harry!
