To enter a password, move the box cursor to select the appropriate letters, and
the X Button to enter those letters (one at a time) until the password is
complete. Select "End" and press the X Button to enter that password. If your
password is valid, you will begin racing in the first race of the country the
password accesses (i.e., if you enter the password for Germany, you will begin
on the first race in Germany).

Note: — Be very careful when you write down your passwords! If one letter is
wrong, the password will NOT work.
