Control
There are two Control options: With Momentum and fo Momentum.

With Momentum is the default control setting. Your helicopter carries
momentum ~ that is, its forward motion is so fast it cannot stop “on a dime”
at the instant you let up on the controls — and you have to compensate for it
while flying.

When you use fo Momentum controls, the Mohican stops immediately when
you let up on the Control Pad.
