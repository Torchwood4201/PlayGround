fundamental moves: kicks, punches, crouches, jumps and 
blocks. These moves may seem trivial compared to powerful 
and acrobatic moves like the flying kick, but knowing how to 
stop, avoid or counteract a flying kick can be far more 
useful than knowing how to land one yourself, Combined, 
these fundamentals are the foundation for both a strong 
defense and a potent offense.
