Prepare yourself for competition! You've 
been selected to represent your country in the 
most prestigious athletic event the world has 
ever known ... and the world is lining up to 
challenge you in 10 Olympic events. You'll 
compete in track, field and shooting events 
ranging from the 100 metre Sprint to the 
Archery competition as the world watches its 
best athletes display their physical superiority! 


Bring all the drama and excitement of the 
Olympic Games home in time for the Atlanta 
1996 Olympic Summer Games. Give it your 
best ... The world is watching! 


In the Olympic Summer Games, you can 
choose to represent one of 32 nations in 
competition, as well as participating in any 
number of events or only the events at which 
-you excel. The events include: 100m Sprint, 
110m Hurdles, Pole Vault, Long Jump, Triple 
Jump, Javelin, High Jump, Discus, Clay 
Pigeon Shooting (Skeet) and Archery.
