Getting Started 


1) Make sure the power is switched OFF. 

2) Insert the Yogi Bear Game Pak as described in 
your SUPER NINTENDO ENTERTAINMENT 
SYSTEM™ manual. 


3) Turn the power switch ON. 


4) After Yogi appears you will see the main title 
screen. 


5) Press START to begin. 


Use the Control Pad to move Yogi left and right. Note 
that as you hold a direction down Yogi begins to run 
faster up to a maximum speed. All levels start at the left 
edge and Yogi must move to the right. 


Press Down on the Control Pad to duck. 


Press B Button to jump. The faster Yogi is running the 
further he can jump.
