Game Play Modes
You can play Donkey Kong Country solo or
with a friend. In two-player CONTEST mode,
you alternate with a friend to see who can fin-
ish the most levels in the shortest time. In

» two-player team mode, both players alternate
control during the game, with one controlling
Donkey Kong and the other controlling Diddy
Kong. This is a great way for a younger player
to play along with an older, more experi-
enced player.
