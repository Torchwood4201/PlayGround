VoxelCraft

Procedural voxel terrain in the browser. Simplex / Perlin fBm heightmaps, tiled streaming mesher, six tree species, blocky clouds, full day/night cycle.

Left-drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Tune the sidebar and press Generate.

Controls

Click canvas to lock mouse · WASD move · Ctrl sprint (uses food) · Ctrl under a 1-block gap to crawl · Space jump/swim · Left-click attack / hold to mine · Right-click place / eat / open furnace · E inventory · 1-9 / wheel select · ESC unlock · I: this panel.
