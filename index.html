🤖 AI LAB — SYSTEM 1 + SYSTEM 2

Two fully separate AI systems. System 1 drives player 1, System 2 drives player 2 — each with its own switch, tuning panel, profile, governor memory and stuck logic. Neither touches the other.

HOW IT LEARNS

  • Watches the game as 32×32 + 64×64 grayscale screenshots, 4 frames of history, every 5th frame.
  • Stores what it sees in a 250k-entry replay memory, then trains on random batches.
  • Epsilon-greedy policy: random moves 80% of the time at first, down to 10% by action 400k.
  • ~6.6M parameters across three branches: action history, screenshot history, current screenshot + spatial transformer.

MANUAL BRAIN (AI LEARNING)

The archive feeds the pilots, not people. On every boot the AI matches the loaded ROM to its manual, loads the cached control lines (or fetches + extracts them once), and uses them to steer: menu confirms, run/block habits, and profile pick. Source: Internet Archive SNES collection, 863 booklets.

Auto-loads on boot. Your click is the approval for a fresh fetch.

LEARN FROM EVERYTHING

Record play sessions and the app captures all three streams together, timestamped: 32×32 grayscale frames (5fps, the DQN paper's input shape), every button press with its source (pad / keyboard / autopilot / replay), and sound energy from the game's audio. Episodes live in your browser — replay any run through the real controllers, or export the JSON dataset for offline training.
