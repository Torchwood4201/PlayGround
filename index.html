— Nuestra Historia —

Two brothers. One recipe. A whole city fed.

Founded in Michoacán and raised in Albuquerque, the Hermanos started with a roadside grill, a cast-iron fryer, and their abuela's spice blend. Forty years later we still marinate every thigh, breast and wing for a full day, bread by hand, and greet every guest like family.

  • Family-owned & operated — no shortcuts, ever
  • Fresh, never frozen New Mexico chicken
  • Community-first: 200+ local fundraisers a year
